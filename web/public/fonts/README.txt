These are the faces the pictures on the site's first screen are drawn in. They
are the launch film's own drawing code (src/film/film.js), which sets its type
in them. All are Latin subsets, or a handful of glyphs, converted to WOFF2.

display-600, display-700, display-800, text-400, text-500, text-600, text-700:
Inter and Inter Display, by Rasmus Andersson. SIL Open Font License 1.1.

chess: the twelve chess pieces of Noto Sans Symbols 2, by the Noto Project
Authors. SIL Open Font License 1.1.

mark: five glyphs of Arimo Bold, by Steve Matteson for Google. Apache
License 2.0.

The words of each license are in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, which is served at
/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md wherever this site is.
