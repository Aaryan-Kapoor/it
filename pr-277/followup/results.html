What the two page-opening measurements mean

The original timing includes Playwright waiting before it clicks and polling after the page is ready. DOM click to state measures the actual captured click until the page receives runtime state. The original metric is retained for comparison. Grid interactivity requires 50 cards and a successful sort, rather than a paint measurement.

The candidate bundle is byte-for-byte unchanged from the original PR. The follow-up fixes the CSP test to inspect served text and investigates the reported regression. The controlled gzip/identity experiment found overlapping load and grid timings. Absolute times from the earlier, busier run are not additional product gains. All samples remain, including one candidate outlier of 2.89 seconds for restart and 3.29 seconds for grid readiness. No local speedup or tail-latency equivalence is claimed.
